Privacy Policy

MPLH 26: Tower Top 3D
Effective date: September 21, 2026

MPLH 26: Tower Top 3D is designed to work without an account and, in its current release configuration, does not collect or transmit personal information to the developer.

1. Information We Collect

We do not require account registration, login, name, email address, phone number, precise location, contacts, photos, camera access, microphone access, or other personal information to play the game.

The current version of the game does not include advertising SDKs, analytics SDKs, or developer-operated tracking systems.

2. Local Game Data

The game stores certain gameplay information locally on your device, such as level progress, settings, high scores, selected Free Mode tower count, music preferences, and sound-effect preferences.

This local data is used only to provide game functionality and is not transmitted to the developer by the game.

3. Sharing Features

The game may provide a Share feature. Sharing occurs only when you choose to use it. The game may open your device's sharing interface, web browser, or a third-party service such as a social network or messaging service.

Once you choose to use a third-party service, that service may process information according to its own privacy policy. We do not control the privacy practices of third-party services.

4. Store and Platform Services

The game may be distributed through platforms such as Apple App Store or Google Play. Those platform providers may independently process information related to downloads, purchases, device security, crash diagnostics, or account activity under their own privacy policies.

5. Children

We do not knowingly collect personal information from children through the game. Because the current version does not include account registration, advertising, analytics, or developer-operated data collection, the game does not request personal information from players.

If a future version introduces features that collect personal information, this Privacy Policy will be updated before those features are released.

6. Data Retention and Deletion

Gameplay progress and preferences are stored locally on the device. You can generally remove this local data by clearing the app's data where supported by the operating system or by uninstalling the app.

Because the current version does not transmit this local gameplay data to the developer, the developer does not maintain a remote copy of that data.

7. Security

We aim to minimize privacy risk by avoiding unnecessary collection of personal information. No method of electronic storage or transmission is completely secure, but the current version is designed so that normal gameplay does not require personal data to be sent to the developer.

GitHub Privacy Statement: https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement
